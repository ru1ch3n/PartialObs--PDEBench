# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in PDE-OBS a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience; and
- focusing on what is best for the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
  and
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits,
code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with
this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual
officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to
bill.xu.github@gmail.com. The project team will review and investigate reports
promptly and fairly. The identity of a reporter will be kept confidential
except where disclosure is required to protect affected people or comply with
law.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will use the following Community Impact Guidelines when deciding
the consequences of an action:

1. **Correction** — For inappropriate language or other behavior judged
   unprofessional, provide a private written warning explaining the violation.
2. **Warning** — For a violation through a single incident or series of
   actions, prohibit interaction with the people involved for a specified time.
3. **Temporary ban** — For a serious violation or sustained inappropriate
   behavior, temporarily prohibit public or private interaction with the
   community.
4. **Permanent ban** — For a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression,
   permanently prohibit public interaction with the community.
